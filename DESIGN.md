# Casualci visual direction

## Product and audience

Serbian menswear storefront with a collection-led home page and full product pages for people browsing a small, edited selection of Ralph Lauren, Stone Island and C.P. Company. The first viewport identifies the clothes and offers a clear path to them; the catalog is the main event.

## Replacement of the rejected design

Removed the oversized wordmark interlude, section numbers, all-caps labels before every heading, orange emphasis in every section and three tall brand cards. The rejected screenshot exposed a scale and rhythm problem at a wide desktop viewport. This version uses a calmer shop hierarchy and a 1480px content measure.

## Tokens

- Background: chalk white `#f7f7f4`
- Content surface: white `#ffffff`
- Text: deep green black `#1b2520`
- Action: dark olive `#30483c`
- Muted text: `#5d6960`
- Rules: `#d7dcd5`

Manrope handles headlines, reading and commerce controls. Barlow Condensed is reserved for the original Casualci wordmark. Large type appears in the hero and main section headings only.

## Composition

The campaign photograph leads with a compact, readable offer and a direct collection link. A narrow logo band carries the three authentic brand assets. The curated product grid follows immediately. One quieter image and story section leads to practical FAQ and a modest footer. Desktop content uses four product columns; mobile keeps two. No decorative full-screen text section separates browsing from buying.

## Interaction and content truth

Filters, search and sorting update the same catalog. Product images and names lead to full pages with size and quantity selection, delivery details, and related items. Basket and favorites use browser storage. Checkout sends a cash-on-delivery request to the owner's Discord webhook; the server recalculates catalog prices. AI product and campaign images are illustrative; real logo sources are in the assets. Availability and shipping are confirmed manually.
