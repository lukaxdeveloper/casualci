## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js on Node.js, intended for Vercel. Catalog is shared between the browser and server. No ChatGPT storage.
## Users
Serbian casual and terrace fashion shoppers seeking Ralph Lauren, Stone Island and related casual menswear.
## Product Purpose
Browse a curated wardrobe, open full product pages, choose a size, save favorites, build a basket and send an order request to the owner's Discord webhook.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Ralph Lauren prices around 5,999 RSD are user supplied; other prices are proposed. No inventory, real product photos, merchant registration details, shipping rate or refund policy was supplied. Customer login is not needed. Checkout sends a cash-on-delivery order request to Discord and must clearly state that availability and shipping are confirmed manually. No database or ChatGPT storage. Serbian Latin copy.
## Brand Commitments
casualci.rs; casual / terrace menswear including Ralph Lauren and Stone Island. Build directly, as requested.
